Uninterrupted Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Uninterrupted does not collect any data. Everything the app records stays on your iPhone, on your Apple Watch and in Apple Health, under your control. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and does not connect to the internet.

This policy explains what Uninterrupted, a meditation app for iPhone and Apple Watch made by Corbin Hesse, does with information about you.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your sessions. When you finish a meditation session of a minute or more, the app saves when it ended and how long it lasted. Shorter sessions are not saved.

This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not synced to iCloud by the app.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app saves each finished session of a minute or more to Apple Health as a Mind and Body workout. It does not save Mindful Minutes. The workout records when the session started and ended and how long it lasted.

The app also asks for permission to read your heart rate. When you run a session on your Apple Watch, the app reads your heart rate during the session, shows it on the watch, and saves it with the workout. The iPhone has no heart rate sensor, so sessions run on the iPhone alone have no heart rate. The app reads no other Health data.

Health data is never sent anywhere by the app, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with or sold to anyone. You can turn the app's access on or off at any time in the Health app, or under Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Live Activities

While a session runs, the app can show the elapsed time on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This display is handled by iOS on your device and contains only the time your session began. You can turn Live Activities off for the app in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.
  • It does not require an account and does not connect to the internet.

Deleting your data

Deleting Uninterrupted from your iPhone removes your session history and everything else the app stored. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. Workouts from earlier versions of the app were saved as Mindful Minutes; those stay until you delete them there too.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
