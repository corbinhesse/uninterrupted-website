Changelog

What changed in each TestFlight build of Uninterrupted, newest first.

Version 0.47.1

Build 4 · November 6, 2026

Features

  • Tap the time during a session to pause it, and tap it again to carry on. Paused time isn’t counted in the session’s length.
  • On the Apple Watch, “Paused” shows in place of the heart rate, and the pause button on the watch’s Smart Stack card works. The card’s name is lilac now, not green.
  • In Light mode, the logo, the timer and the Sessions and Settings titles are a deeper purple, and most of the black and grey is purple now.
  • In Light mode, Settings’ Apple Health and Show Introduction labels, the appearance names and the Privacy Policy link are purple too, and the intro’s last page has a lighter purple line.
  • Settings shows Apple Health as one card: a tick when sessions are saved to it, and a button that opens the Health app. There is no separate Apple Health page any more.
  • The three appearance choices in Settings are square.
  • The watch face has a third Uninterrupted complication, for the wide slot: this week’s sessions, a circle for each day, with the two weeks before underneath. A tap opens the app on Begin.
  • The watch face has a second Uninterrupted complication: the Begin button in miniature. A tap opens the app on Begin.
  • Swipe right from Begin for the new Sessions screen: a chart of This Week, 4 Weeks or Months, and each session’s length and when it began, read back from Apple Health (which asks once more for permission).
  • Settings is now a swipe left from Begin instead of a sheet, with dots at the bottom showing which screen you’re on. Beta Testing and the list of sessions are gone from Settings, and the night sky is darker.

Fixes

  • The Privacy Policy link at the bottom of Settings opens the policy on the new website, uninterruptedmeditation.com.
  • The This Week complication on the watch shows the same sessions as the Sessions screen on the iPhone, including the two weeks underneath.
  • In Dark mode, the circles behind the icons on the introduction are the same coral-pink tint as the week row on Sessions, and so are the Apple Health and Show Introduction cards in Settings.
  • The week on the watch face starts on the same day as First Day of Week on the iPhone. Open the iPhone app and then the watch app once after changing it.
  • The day letters on the This Week complication are a little lighter.
  • On the watch, the Uninterrupted complication is a quiet grey disc with the logo in the Begin button’s colors, not a bright pink disc.
  • On the watch, Begin sits a little lower, with more room under the time at the top.
  • The “Allow Live Activities from Uninterrupted?” prompt is white in Light mode instead of pink.

Version 0.41.5

Build 3 · October 29, 2026

Features

  • In the introduction, the heart button now stays in place on the way to the last page, and its heart draws itself into the Begin icon instead of the button dropping off the screen.

Fixes

  • On the Apple Watch, “Uninterrupted” on the workout card in the Smart Stack is lilac instead of green.
  • On the Apple Watch, the clock at the top of the session screen is centered again on watchOS 27.
  • On the Apple Watch, the X on Done now turns into the check right at one minute, not a few seconds late.
  • On the Apple Watch, the heart rate no longer shows during a session if Apple Health access was turned off.
  • The session timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island now uses the same rounded font as the timer in the app.

Version 0.40.0

Build 2 · October 27, 2026

Features

  • New Apple Watch complication: the sunrise logo opens Uninterrupted, ready to Begin. Add it to a watch face from the face’s Edit screen.

Fixes

  • A session you end on Apple Watch now ends on your iPhone too, even if the iPhone was locked, and is saved to Apple Health once instead of twice.
