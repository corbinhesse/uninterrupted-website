Changelog

What changed in each TestFlight build of Uninterrupted, newest first.

Version 0.41.5

Build 3 · October 29, 2026

Features

  • In the introduction, the heart button now stays in place on the way to the last page, and its heart draws itself into the Begin icon instead of the button dropping off the screen.

Fixes

  • On the Apple Watch, “Uninterrupted” on the workout card in the Smart Stack is lilac instead of green.
  • On the Apple Watch, the clock at the top of the session screen is centered again on watchOS 27.
  • On the Apple Watch, the X on Done now turns into the check right at one minute, not a few seconds late.
  • On the Apple Watch, the heart rate no longer shows during a session if Apple Health access was turned off.
  • The session timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island now uses the same rounded font as the timer in the app.

Version 0.40.0

Build 2 · October 27, 2026

Features

  • New Apple Watch complication: the sunrise logo opens Uninterrupted, ready to Begin. Add it to a watch face from the face’s Edit screen.

Fixes

  • A session you end on Apple Watch now ends on your iPhone too, even if the iPhone was locked, and is saved to Apple Health once instead of twice.

Fixes

  • Fixes the build so it can be sent through TestFlight.
